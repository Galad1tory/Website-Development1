Who I am

Hi! My name is Serhii Rozvorovych. I work as a cook in a fast-food restaurant and study cybersecurity. I’m into music, travel, a bit of tech, and I enjoy tinkering with cars and their electronics. I’m usually calm, sometimes energetic, I love joking with friends, and I’m always up for a silly but fun idea. My dream is to write my own music and release a full album.

My Story

One of the most memorable experiences I’ve had was a road trip across Europe with my brother — from Ireland by ferry to France, then Paris, Prague, Warsaw, Berlin, Amsterdam, and Brussels. Warsaw felt the closest to home — the people and mentality reminded me of Ukraine. When I think back to childhood, I remember endless bike rides with a friend; we tried cool tricks and usually ended up falling and laughing. I’ve changed a lot over time — from a shy kid to an open person who enjoys life, friends, and new challenges.
